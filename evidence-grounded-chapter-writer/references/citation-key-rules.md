# Citation Key And Source Rules

## Working Citation Markers

- `[参考文献]`: formal citation placeholder inside the chapter draft.
- `[引用关键词: 检索词]`: local library or Zotero evidence note.
- `[网络资料: 检索词或网页标题]`: web evidence note.
- `[用户材料: 文件名或材料名]`: user-provided material.
- `[待补来源: 证据类型]`: unsupported claim that should not be finalized.

## Title-Based Keys

- Chinese title: keep the original title exactly.
- English title: lowercase the title, remove spaces and punctuation.
- Duplicate English key: append year or first author.

Examples:

- `中文论文原标题`
- `sampleenglishtitle`
- `sampleenglishtitle2021`

## Writing Constraints

Do not convert source notes into formal bibliography entries unless the source metadata is available. Do not fabricate authors, journals, years, DOI, standards numbers, or equipment parameters.

When evidence is missing, write a cautious design statement or mark `[待补来源: ...]`.
